About this analysis
10,430 rows across 4 Reddit-sourced admission cycles (2022–23 through 2025–26)
Scraped from r/OntarioUniversities, normalized and deduplicated. 2025–26 cycle finalized May 2026.
~95%+ of Reddit reports are accepted applicants — all percentiles are relative to the admitted population, not the full applicant pool
Real acceptance rates are lower; this data cannot show the full rejection distribution.
OOP samples are small (n=4–9 per Tier-1 program) — treat floors as directional, not hard thresholds
The 98.0% OOP McMaster rejection is one data point. It is meaningful, not definitive.
2026–27 supp app questions will differ from 2025–26 — prepare for the format, not specific prompts
Programs rotate questions annually. Analytical readiness and rehearsed narrative transfer across cycles.
